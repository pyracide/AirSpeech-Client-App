## The Real Root Cause: Missing SDIO Bus Power (LDO)

After extensive research, I found the actual problem. Here's what's happening:

### Why `sdmmc_host_clock_update_command` returns `0x107`

The ESP32-P4's SDMMC controller needs its **I/O voltage domain** (`VDDPST_5` / `SD_VREF`) to be powered before it can function. On the P4-EYE, this voltage is supplied by an **internal on-chip LDO** (channel 4).

This tells ESP-Hosted to initialize the on-chip LDO channel 4 to supply 3.3V to the SDMMC I/O pins before attempting any bus communication.
